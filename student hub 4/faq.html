FAQs

1. What is Student Hub?

Student Hub is an online platform that provides study materials, previous year papers, important notices and useful academic resources for students.

2. Is Student Hub free to use?

Yes. Student Hub is completely free for all students.

3. Can I download notes?

Yes. Students can easily access and download notes whenever required.

4. How can I get previous year question papers?

Visit the Dashboard section and select the subject to access previous year papers.

5. How can I contact support?

You can contact the college administration or visit the About page for more information.
